 WINDING AND ASSEMBLY 
 SYSTEMS FOR 
 MOTORS AND COILS

 Since over 80 years we stand out for our research and technological innovations in winding and assembly systems for motors and coils, where precision, flexibility and customization are mandatory. 

We cover different industries such as automotive, appliances, electromechanical applications, RFID, consumer electronics, and more. We are

innovators 

 Our coil winding technology includes two main categories: spindle winding and flyer winding.

We deliver machines and systems that go from a single spindle up to 24 spindles, using wire size from 0,02 to 2,5 mm and up to 25.000 RPM. 

 We are

innovators We master several different motor winding methods: spindle winding and flyer winding, developed from our experience in coil, and needle winding on open and closed stators, developed specifically for motor winding.
